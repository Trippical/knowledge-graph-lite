# Ops Manager

Owns day-to-day operations, budgets and sign-offs. Approval authority for
refunds over £500 per [refund-approvals.md](refund-approvals.md). Owns the
onboarding process for new starters.

## Delegation cover (1–31 March)

Per the delegation memo: while the role holder is on leave from 1 to 31
March, all sign-offs and approvals are delegated to Marcus Webb, who has
full authority on operational decisions while the cover is in place.
Role holders and cover names are recorded here as facts from the memo —
people are not entities in this library; roles are.
